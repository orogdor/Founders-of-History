- grass: Grass001 — https://ambientcg.com/a/Grass001
- soil: Ground048 — https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground048
- forest: Ground023 — https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground023
- gravel roads: Gravel001 — https://ambientcg.com/a/Gravel001
- stone paving: PavingStones036 — https://ambientcg.com/a/PavingStones036
- asphalt roads: Asphalt004 — https://ambientcg.com/a/Asphalt004

Source packages: 1K-JPG. The original grass, soil and forest color, NormalGL
and roughness maps use 1024 × 1024 WebP. The displacement maps and the road color
and NormalGL maps use 512 × 512 WebP. Displacement and road normal maps are lossless.
The ground shader samples grass, soil and forest displacement from the red, green
and blue channels of `ground-height.webp` respectively.
These textures can be modified and included in a commercial game under CC0.
